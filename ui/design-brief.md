# Design context brief — for Claude (UI)

Context only. This tells you what the product is and who it's for — not what to build or how.

## The product, in one line
An AI wealth-advisory companion — shown as a friendly avatar — that lives inside IDBI Bank's
mobile app and helps everyday customers make sense of their money by talking to it.
(Working name "Asha / ArthSakhi" — treat as placeholder.)

## Who it's for
- IDBI's mass-retail customers, heavily tier-2 / tier-3 India. Many are first-time or nervous
  investors with low financial confidence, more comfortable in Hindi or a regional language
  than in English.
- They trust the bank. They don't trust "finance apps." So the companion has to feel like a
  helpful person from an institution they already rely on — a *saathi* (companion), not a
  slick fintech product.

## Where it lives
- It's a module *inside* IDBI's existing banking app, not a standalone app. It should feel
  like it belongs to the bank, while being its own calm, focused space once you're in it.
- Mobile, one-handed, often on modest phones and uneven networks.

## The interaction idea
- Concierge, not self-serve. The customer isn't hunting through dashboards and menus — they're
  in a guided conversation with an advisor who leads. The person responds more than they navigate.
- Voice matters as much as text: many users will speak, in their own language, rather than type.

## The backdrop that never changes
- The bank is a distributor, not a registered advisor — the posture is "here's what suits you,"
  never a hard, authoritative "this is the advice."
- Real money and personal data are involved. The experience must feel safe, unhurried, and
  transparent — never pushy, never gamified.

## The feeling to aim for
Warm, human, trustworthy, calm, premium-but-accessible. Quiet confidence. The opposite of a
busy trading screen.

## The screen in focus — "Converse"
This is the heart of the product: the moment a customer is actually talking to their wealth
companion — asking a money question in their own words and getting back an answer that feels
clear, personal, and human. Everything else in the product exists to get people to this moment
and to help them act on what happens here.

One thing that makes this companion different: it *remembers*. It can gently refer back to
things the customer shared before, so the conversation feels like a continuing relationship,
not a fresh transaction each time.
