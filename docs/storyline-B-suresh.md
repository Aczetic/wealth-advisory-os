## 2. The core advisory story (the pitch, quantified)

₹28K/month for 3 years, then ₹40K once the car EMI ends (if redirected, not absorbed):
- at FD/PO ~6.5% → **~₹1Cr**
- goal-matched (equity glide for far goals, debt/FD for near) ~10% blended → **~₹1.35Cr+**

**Same savings, right vehicles.** Asha never asks him to gamble: son's fees stay in
FD/debt (his comfort zone, validated); only far-dated money (daughter's wedding,
retirement) takes equity risk, gliding to safety as each goal approaches. Each goal
gets its own bucket + glide path — "alag dabba har sapne ke liye" framing.
